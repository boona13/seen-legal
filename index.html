SEEN

Privacy Policy (Beta)

Effective 28 September 2026

SEEN is a camera-first app for sharing photos, videos, stories and messages. This beta is run by Ibrahim Boona, an individual developer. This policy explains what the beta collects, why, and what you can do about it. Questions: boonamazon@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address, username, display name and password. Passwords are stored only as a secure hash.
  • Profile: anything you add, such as a bio, profile photo, cover photo, location text or website.
  • Content: posts, stories, Snaps, chat messages, comments, reactions and the photos, videos and voice messages you share.
  • Location (optional): only if you allow location access and choose to tag a post or story. We store the coordinates and place name with that item.
  • Notifications: if you allow notifications, your device's push token and platform (iOS or Android).
  • Safety records: people you block or mute, and reports you file.

The camera, microphone and photo library are used only when you capture or pick something. Nothing is uploaded unless you post or send it.

What we don't do

No advertising, no selling or renting your data, and no third-party analytics or tracking tools in the app.

How we use it

To run SEEN: showing your profile and posts, delivering messages and Snaps, sending the notifications you've turned on, and keeping people safe through blocks and reports.

Who can see your content

Posts and your profile are visible to other SEEN users according to your settings. Chats and Snaps are visible only to the people in that conversation. Stories expire after 24 hours.

Service providers

  • Supabase hosts our database, file storage and sign-in, in the EU (Frankfurt, Germany).
  • Expo and Apple deliver push notifications. Apple also distributes the beta through TestFlight.
  • Apple Maps turns coordinates into place names on your device when you tag a location.

They process data only to provide these services to us.

Keeping and deleting your data

You can delete your own posts, stories and messages at any time. Deleting your account in Settings removes your account, profile and content from our database. During the beta, photo and video files you uploaded are not yet removed automatically when you delete your account; email us and we'll delete them. Beta data may be wiped when the beta ends.

Your choices and rights

You can turn off location and notifications in your device settings at any time. To ask for a copy of your data, a correction or deletion, email boonamazon@gmail.com.

Children

SEEN isn't intended for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and tell beta testers in the app or by email for any significant change.
