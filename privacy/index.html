ULOOM

Privacy Policy (Beta)

Effective 6 October 2026

ULOOM is a camera-first app for sharing photos, videos, stories and messages. This beta is run by Ibrahim Boona, an individual developer. This policy explains what the beta collects, why, and what you can do about it. Questions: boonamazon@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address, username, display name and password. Passwords are stored only as a secure hash. If you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive the email address (or Apple's private relay address) and name they share with us instead of a password.
  • Profile: anything you add, such as a bio, profile photo, cover photo, location text or website.
  • Content: posts, stories, Snaps, chat messages, comments, reactions and the photos, videos and voice messages you share.
  • When your photo or video was taken: photos and videos in ULOOM come from its camera. When you post or send one, we store when it was taken, as your phone's clock reported it, and which edits it went through (for example a trim, a filter or text on top). It's used to order the angles in a Scene and to show when something was filmed, for example "Captured 3 Oct" on a post that went out later. It is never a place.
  • Your archive (if you use it): captures you choose to keep in your private Archive, and, if you turn it on, copies of the captures you post to Watch, Reports or Stories, each with the time it was taken. Only you can see your archive. It stays after a post is deleted or a Story ends, until you delete the item or your account. Moments are never kept there.
  • Location (optional): only if you allow location access and choose to tag a post or story. See Location below.
  • What Watch showed you: when you use Watch, discover Stories or open a Scene, we keep a record of which posts and Stories we showed you and in what order, so you don't see the same ones again and so we can check how recommendations work. See Usage data below.
  • Usage data (optional): only if you turn on "Share usage data" in Settings. See Usage data below.
  • Notifications: if you allow notifications, your device's push token and platform (iOS or Android).
  • Safety records: people you block or mute, and reports you file.
  • Edits and screenshots: when you edit a post, report update, comment or message, we keep that it was edited and when (it shows as "Edited"). When someone screenshots a moment you sent them, the chat shows that they did.

The camera and microphone are used only when you capture something. ULOOM doesn't read your photo library; it only adds to it when you save a photo or video to your camera roll. Nothing is uploaded unless you post, send or archive it.

Location

  • We don't store your exact position. When you tag a post or story, your device uses your exact position only to find place names and to work out the map pin. What we store is the centre of the area you're in: about 1 km across if you choose Neighbourhood (the default), or about 25 km across if you choose City. Our servers apply the same rounding again before anything is saved.
  • The place name is your choice. We store the name you pick, such as a district, a city, a street or venue, or text you type, together with which kind of name it is and the precision you chose. A street, venue or typed name can say more about where you were than the map pin does, and it's shown with your post as you chose it. When you choose a street, venue or typed name, or a district name with the City pin, the app points this out before you use it.
  • Near me: when you use Near me on the Map, the app sends the centre of your roughly 1 km area, not your exact position. It's used for that search only and isn't stored.
  • On the Map: for people who don't follow you, public stories appear only in areas where at least three people have stories they can see. Popular places lists only district and city names used by at least three people in public posts.
  • Place names on your device: the app asks your phone's operating system (on iPhone, Apple Maps) to turn your exact position into place names. That lookup can involve Apple's servers and is covered by Apple's privacy policy, not ours.
  • Never elsewhere: usage data and the record of what Watch showed you never include your location. A Scene shows only the place names its public posts already show.
  • Earlier posts: posts and stories tagged before 30 September 2026 stored exact positions. We've replaced them with the rounded 1 km area. We keep a copy of those earlier exact positions until 14 October 2026, out of reach of the app and of every user, so the change can be undone if something goes wrong; the copy is then deleted, and deleting a post or story deletes its copy too. Our hosting provider's backups may still contain older exact positions until those backups expire.
  • Inside video files: see Photo and video files.

Photo and video files

  • Photos are re-encoded on your device before upload. The uploaded file keeps only its size and orientation, not the camera details or location your phone may have stored in the original.
  • Videos posted or sent from the current beta version are rewritten on your iPhone before upload, removing the details cameras store inside the file, such as the place it was recorded, the device, the lens and the recording time. If that can't be done, the video isn't uploaded. If you trim a video, only the part you keep is uploaded. Muting a video mutes it when it plays in ULOOM, but its sound is still in the uploaded file. Sharing videos from Android isn't available yet.
  • Earlier videos: videos uploaded with earlier beta versions may still contain those details: the device model, lens and recording time, and, for a video recorded with your phone's Camera app with location turned on, the place where it was recorded. A video trimmed in an earlier beta version was uploaded in full, including the parts you cut. Anyone who can open the file can read or watch them. We plan to clean these files; until then, email us and we'll delete any of your earlier videos.
  • Web addresses of files: photos and videos in posts and stories, and profile and cover photos, are stored as files with long, hard-to-guess web addresses. The app shows them only to people allowed to see the post or story, but anyone who has a file's address can open it, including files of followers-only posts, stories shared with a limited audience, and expired stories. People can't browse or list other users' files. Chat photos, videos and voice messages are private: only people in the conversation can open them, through links that expire.

Usage data

  • What Watch showed you: to run Watch, Stories discovery and Scenes, we keep a record of the posts and Stories we showed you, in order, with how each was picked. It's kept for 60 days, whether or not you share usage data, and deleted with your account.
  • Share usage data (optional, off until you turn it on): if you turn on "Share usage data" in Settings, we record how you use the app so we can improve it: which posts, Stories and Scene angles were shown to you, how long you watched, and what you did with them (for example like, save, comment, share, follow, hide or report). Each record is linked to your account, a random session number and the app version. Usage records are kept for 90 days and deleted with your account. You can turn it off at any time and collection stops straight away.
  • Neither includes your location, and neither is shared with anyone else.

What we don't do

No advertising, no selling or renting your data, and no third-party analytics or tracking tools in the app. Usage data, if you share it, stays with ULOOM.

How we use it

To run ULOOM: showing your profile and posts, choosing what Watch shows you, delivering messages and Snaps, sending the notifications you've turned on, and keeping people safe through blocks and reports. If you share usage data, also to improve the app.

Who can see your content

Posts and your profile are visible to other ULOOM users according to your settings. Chats and Snaps are visible only to the people in that conversation. Stories expire after 24 hours.

Private account: if you turn it on in Settings, only people you accept can add you and see your posts, Stories and friends. Anyone can still find your name, username, profile photo and bio, and the people who already follow you stay. Your posts don't join Scenes and can't be reshared. When you turn it off, people who asked to add you become followers.

Scenes group public posts of the same moment, added by their authors. Only public posts can join, and anyone who can see your post can see it in the Scene, with your name, profile photo, the place name on your post and when it was filmed. The Scene shows who started it. You can remove your angle at any time; a post that is deleted or no longer public leaves every Scene.

Service providers

  • Supabase hosts our database, file storage and sign-in, in the EU (Frankfurt, Germany).
  • Expo and Apple deliver push notifications. Apple also distributes the beta through TestFlight.
  • Apple and Google handle sign-in if you choose to sign in with them.
  • Apple Maps turns your position into place names on your device when you tag a location.
  • OpenRouter passes requests from the Director in Formats to an AI model made by OpenAI, which writes the script. OpenRouter sends each request to a provider that runs that model, and we ask it to use only providers that don't keep or train on requests. If you use the Director, the topic you type, the template you picked and the language you chose go from our servers through OpenRouter to that provider; your name, account and location don't.

They process data only to provide these services to us.

Keeping and deleting your data

You can delete your own posts, stories, comments, archive items and messages at any time, and delete a chat or a message for yourself only. Deleting a post or story also deletes its photo and video files, unless the same file is still used somewhere else, such as your archive. Deleting your account in Settings removes your account, profile, content, archive, usage data and Watch records from our database, and the photo and video files you uploaded. If you signed in with Apple, we also ask Apple to remove ULOOM from your Apple ID. Files uploaded with earlier beta versions and left after a deletion can be removed on request: email us. Beta data may be wiped when the beta ends.

Your choices and rights

You can turn off location and notifications in your device settings, and "Share usage data" in ULOOM's Settings, at any time. To get a copy of your data, use Download your data in Settings → Account: it saves one file with your account, profile, posts, Stories, archive, the messages you sent and your activity, with links to your photos, videos and voice notes. It doesn't include other people's messages to you. For a correction or deletion, or anything the download doesn't cover, email boonamazon@gmail.com.

Children

ULOOM isn't intended for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and tell beta testers in the app or by email for any significant change.
