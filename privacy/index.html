ULOOM

Privacy Policy (Beta)

Effective 8 October 2026

ULOOM is a camera-first app for sharing photos, videos, stories and messages. This beta is run by Ibrahim Boona, an individual developer. This policy explains what the beta collects, why, and what you can do about it. Questions: boonamazon@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address, username, display name and password. Passwords are stored only as a secure hash. If you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive the email address (or Apple's private relay address) and name they share with us instead of a password.
  • Your birthday: your date of birth, asked when you sign up (or once, the next time you open ULOOM if you joined before we asked). It's private. See Your birthday below.
  • Profile: anything you add, such as a bio, profile photo, cover photo, location text or website.
  • Content: posts, stories, Snaps, chat messages, comments, reactions and the photos, videos and voice messages you share.
  • When your photo or video was taken: photos and videos in ULOOM come from its camera. When you post or send one, we store when it was taken, as your phone's clock reported it, and which edits it went through (for example a trim, a filter or text on top). It's used to order the angles in a Scene and to show when something was filmed, for example "Captured 3 Oct" on a post that went out later. It is never a place.
  • Your archive (if you use it): captures you choose to keep in your private Archive, and, if you turn it on, copies of the captures you post to Watch, Reports or Stories, each with the time it was taken. Only you can see your archive. It stays after a post is deleted or a Story ends, until you delete the item or your account. Moments are never kept there.
  • Location (optional): only if you allow location access and choose to tag a post or story. See Location below.
  • What Watch showed you: when you use Watch, discover Stories or open a Scene, we keep a record of which posts and Stories we showed you and in what order, so you don't see the same ones again and so we can check how recommendations work. See Usage data below.
  • What For You learns: which creators and topics you seem to like, from what you like, save, comment on and repost, and from "Not interested" and "Show less". If you're an adult with Personalised For You on, also from finishing a video, watching for 10 seconds or more, or skipping in under 2 seconds. We keep the interests, not a list of what you watched. You can turn it off or reset it. See How For You is chosen below.
  • Who we suggested to you: which accounts we suggested you follow and on how many days, so the same ones don't keep coming back. See Suggested people and rising creators below.
  • Views, for Impact: when someone else's post, report, Story or profile is open on your screen for 2 seconds or more, the app tells our servers so its author can see how far it travelled, with the country your phone is set to. We add it to that post's totals and never store who viewed it. See Views and Impact below.
  • Days you use ULOOM: on each day you open ULOOM, the app tells our servers, with your phone type, the app version and the country your phone is set to. We keep the day under a code instead of your account, to count how many people use ULOOM and how many come back. You can turn it off. See Counting how many people use ULOOM below.
  • Usage data (optional): only if you turn on "Share usage data" in Settings. See Usage data below.
  • Notifications: if you allow notifications, your device's push token and platform (iOS or Android).
  • Safety records: people you block or mute, reports you file, and decisions our team makes about your account or what you posted, with their reasons and any appeal. See Reports and our team's decisions.
  • Sign-in records: for security, when you sign in we keep the time, the network address (IP) and the device or browser used, for six months.
  • Edits and screenshots: when you edit a post, report update, comment or message, we keep that it was edited and when (it shows as "Edited"). When someone screenshots a moment you sent them, the chat shows that they did.

The camera and microphone are used only when you capture something. ULOOM doesn't read your photo library; it only adds to it when you save a photo or video to your camera roll. Nothing is uploaded unless you post, send or archive it.

Location

  • We don't store your exact position. When you tag a post or story, your device uses your exact position only to find place names and to work out the map pin. What we store is the centre of the area you're in: about 1 km across if you choose Neighbourhood (the default), or about 25 km across if you choose City. Our servers apply the same rounding again before anything is saved.
  • The place name is your choice. We store the name you pick, such as a district, a city, a street or venue, or text you type, together with which kind of name it is and the precision you chose. A street, venue or typed name can say more about where you were than the map pin does, and it's shown with your post as you chose it. When you choose a street, venue or typed name, or a district name with the City pin, the app points this out before you use it.
  • Near me: when you use Near me on the Map, the app sends the centre of your roughly 1 km area, not your exact position. It's used for that search only and isn't stored.
  • On the Map: for people who don't follow you, public stories appear only in areas where at least three people have stories they can see. Popular places lists only district and city names used by at least three people in public posts.
  • Place names on your device: the app asks your phone's operating system (on iPhone, Apple Maps) to turn your exact position into place names. That lookup can involve Apple's servers and is covered by Apple's privacy policy, not ours.
  • Never elsewhere: usage data and the record of what Watch showed you never include your location. A Scene shows only the place names its public posts already show.
  • Earlier posts: posts and stories tagged before 30 September 2026 stored exact positions. We've replaced them with the rounded 1 km area. We keep a copy of those earlier exact positions until 14 October 2026, out of reach of the app and of every user, so the change can be undone if something goes wrong; the copy is then deleted, and deleting a post or story deletes its copy too. Our hosting provider's backups may still contain older exact positions until those backups expire.
  • Inside video files: see Photo and video files.

Photo and video files

  • Photos are re-encoded on your device before upload. The uploaded file keeps only its size and orientation, not the camera details or location your phone may have stored in the original.
  • Videos posted or sent from the current beta version are rewritten on your iPhone before upload, removing the details cameras store inside the file, such as the place it was recorded, the device, the lens and the recording time. If that can't be done, the video isn't uploaded. If you trim a video, only the part you keep is uploaded. Muting a video mutes it when it plays in ULOOM, but its sound is still in the uploaded file. Sharing videos from Android isn't available yet.
  • Earlier videos: videos uploaded with earlier beta versions may still contain those details: the device model, lens and recording time, and, for a video recorded with your phone's Camera app with location turned on, the place where it was recorded. A video trimmed in an earlier beta version was uploaded in full, including the parts you cut. Anyone who can open the file can read or watch them. We plan to clean these files; until then, email us and we'll delete any of your earlier videos.
  • Web addresses of files: photos and videos in posts and stories, and profile and cover photos, are stored as files with long, hard-to-guess web addresses. The app shows them only to people allowed to see the post or story, but anyone who has a file's address can open it, including files of followers-only posts, stories shared with a limited audience, and expired stories. People can't browse or list other users' files. Chat photos, videos and voice messages are private: only people in the conversation can open them, through links that expire. If someone reports a message, our team can open it to review the report (see Reports and our team's decisions).

Usage data

  • What Watch showed you: to run Watch, Stories discovery and Scenes, we keep a record of the posts and Stories we showed you, in order, with how each was picked. It's kept for 60 days, whether or not you share usage data, and deleted with your account.
  • Share usage data (optional, off until you turn it on): if you turn on "Share usage data" in Settings, we record how you use the app so we can improve it: which posts, Stories and Scene angles were shown to you, how long you watched, and what you did with them (for example like, save, comment, share, follow, hide or report). Each record is linked to your account, a random session number and the app version. Usage records are kept for 90 days and deleted with your account. You can turn it off at any time and collection stops straight away.
  • Neither includes your location, and neither is shared with anyone else.

Your birthday

  • Why we ask: ULOOM is for people 13 and older, and older where the law says so (16 in Australia). We use your birthday to check that, to give teens safer settings (a new account of someone under 18 starts private, and only their followers are shown their posts in recommendations), and, as totals only, to show creators the age ranges of their adult viewers in Impact and to show our team how many people of each age range use ULOOM.
  • Private: it never shows on your profile and isn't shared with anyone. Our team sees your age range when it's needed to keep people safe; only team members with access to account details see the date.
  • The country of your phone: when you give your birthday, we keep the country your phone is set to, because some countries set a higher minimum age, and to count which tags are trending in each country (as totals only; see Hashtags and trends). It's a setting, never a location.
  • Getting it right: you give it once and can't change it yourself. If it's wrong, email us and we'll correct it.
  • Under the minimum age: sign-up stops before an account is made. If an existing account gives a birthday under the minimum, we lock it straight away and delete it, unless you show us the date was a mistake.

Views and Impact

  • What's counted: when someone else's post, report, Story or profile is on your screen for at least 2 seconds, or plays for 2 seconds, the app sends which one it was, where in the app you found it (for example the Map or Watch), how long it was on screen, and whether a video played to the end. We also note whether you follow its author and, if you're an adult, your age range and the country your phone is set to (in its settings, not where you are). The app also counts when you share a post outside ULOOM, open a report's source, or follow someone from their post. Views of your own posts aren't counted.
  • Never who: each view is added to totals for that post and its author, by day and by hour. We don't store who viewed anything. To count each person once a day, we keep a code made from your account and the post with a key that changes every day; the key and the codes are deleted when the day ends (UTC), and after that they can't be traced to anyone. To count how many different people a post reached, we keep a statistical summary (a HyperLogLog sketch) that can estimate a number of people but can't list them. If you're an adult with Personalised For You on, the same view can also teach For You what you like (see How For You is chosen); that keeps no list of views either.
  • Who sees the numbers: only the author, in Impact, and our support team when the author asks for help (every look is recorded). Breakdowns, such as where people found a post, followers or new people, and when people watch, appear only when at least 10 people are in them, so they can't single anyone out. The age ranges and countries of adult viewers appear only as totals, once at least 100 people are in them. Viewers under 18 are never part of any breakdown. Impact never shows gender (ULOOM doesn't collect it) or where anyone is. The list of who viewed your Story, which ULOOM already shows you, stays as it is. Our team also sees views added up across all of ULOOM (for example by part of the app, topic, country or adult age range), as totals only.
  • Likes, comments, reposts, saves, sends and follows on your posts and Stories are also added up by day for Impact, from what ULOOM already records.
  • How long: daily totals are kept for 13 months. Deleting a post deletes its totals, and deleting your account deletes the totals for your posts, Stories and profile. Views you added to other people's posts stay in their totals, since nothing there links them to you.
  • This counting is part of how ULOOM works, so it runs for everyone. It isn't the optional "Share usage data", which stays separate, and it never includes your location.

Counting how many people use ULOOM

  • What's kept: the days (UTC) you open ULOOM, your phone type (iPhone or Android), the app version, the country your phone is set to (a setting, not where you are), your age range from your birthday, and the day you joined.
  • Under a code, not your account: we keep it under a code made from your account with a secret key, so our team can't tell whose it is. They only see totals, such as how many people opened ULOOM yesterday or how many of the people who joined last week came back. Groups of fewer than 10 people aren't shown.
  • Never: what you did or watched in the app, where you are, or who you are.
  • How long: 13 months, and deleted with your account.
  • Turn it off: Settings → Privacy → Count my visits. Off deletes what was kept and stops it straight away.

How For You is chosen

  • What it uses: how people engage with each post (as totals, never who), how new it is, who and which topics you follow, and what For You has learned you like. Posts from the country your phone is set to rank a little higher. Every post is checked against what you may see first: your blocks and mutes, "Not interested", the rules for under-18s and our team's limits.
  • What it learns: which creators and topics you seem to like, from what you like, save, comment on and repost, and from "Not interested" and "Show less from @name". If you're an adult with Personalised For You on, also from finishing a video, watching for 10 seconds or more, or skipping in under 2 seconds. It never learns from what anyone under 18 watches.
  • What we keep: your interest in up to 200 creators and topics, each fading by half every 30 days. It isn't a history of what you watched: a view that teaches For You is added to your interests within minutes and then deleted. Your interests are deleted when you turn Personalised For You off, reset For You, or delete your account. We also count how many times each post was shown in Watch, as totals, never who.
  • Your controls: in Settings → Recommendations you can turn Personalised For You off (For You then uses only who and which topics you follow, not what it learned or your country), reset it, and see who you've chosen to see less of. "Not interested" and "Show less" are in each post's menu.
  • New posts and new people: a new post is shown to a small number of people first and reaches more if they like it. A new person's first posts get a first audience of real people. We never add fake views, likes or followers.
  • Following: until you follow 10 people, Following adds posts you might like after your friends' ones, marked "Suggested for you".
  • Why am I seeing this? In a post's menu in Watch, this shows the reason we noted when we chose it for you (for example "You follow @name", "It's popular on ULOOM right now", "It's a new post we're showing to a few people first"), with what's true now, such as a topic of it that you follow. Only you can see the reasons for what was shown to you.
  • First posts: when you post for the first time, the people who follow you and can see the post are told ("posted for the first time. Say welcome"). A day later we tell you how many people it reached, from the same totals as Impact. Like other notifications, they show in Notifications; Settings → Notifications → First posts turns off their push notifications.

Suggested people and rising creators

  • Who we suggest to you: people who follow you, people followed by people you follow, creators For You has learned you like, popular creators in topics you follow, accounts popular with people in your country, and rising creators our team has checked. Each suggestion says why. With Personalised For You off, we don't use what For You learned or your country (rising creators are then only those rising everywhere).
  • Who we never suggest: people you follow or asked to follow, people you blocked or who blocked you, people you muted or see less of, private and limited accounts, accounts less than a week old, and anyone under 18. People under 18 are only suggested adults who post in topics they follow.
  • What we keep: which accounts we suggested to you and on how many days. Someone you passed over on 3 days isn't suggested to you for 30 days, and someone you remove isn't suggested for 90. Kept for 60 days after a suggestion was last shown, and deleted with either account.
  • Your account in suggestions: Settings → Recommendations → "Suggest my account to others". Off: you aren't suggested to anyone and aren't shown as a rising creator; people can still find you by search and through your posts. It's off, and can't be turned on, for anyone under 18 (or without a birthday).
  • "Popular in your country" is worked out from the totals of where the people watching an account are (the country their phones are set to), never from the account's own country.
  • Rising creators: adults whose public accounts are at least a week old, with fewer than 10,000 followers, growing faster than their own usual. We count new followers from accounts at least a week old (not people who followed each other back within a day) and completed views from people who don't follow them, as totals. Someone on our team checks each creator before they're shown, in Discover and as a suggestion. "Rising in Saudi Arabia" means at least 10, and most, of their new followers are there.

What we don't do

No advertising, no selling or renting your data, and no third-party analytics or tracking tools in the app. Usage data, if you share it, stays with ULOOM.

How we use it

To run ULOOM: showing your profile and posts, choosing what Watch shows you, delivering messages and Snaps, sending the notifications you've turned on, showing authors how far their posts and Stories travelled (Impact), and keeping people safe through blocks, reports and our team's review, and protecting accounts with sign-in records. We also add up what happens on ULOOM into totals our team uses to run and plan it, for example how many people joined, posted, watched or came back each day, by country and adult age range. Totals don't identify anyone. They may also help us plan whether advertising could work one day; if ULOOM ever shows ads, this policy will change first and we'll tell you. If you share usage data, also to improve the app.

Who can see your content

Posts and your profile are visible to other ULOOM users according to your settings. Chats and Snaps are visible only to the people in that conversation, except that when someone reports a message, our team can see that message to review the report. Stories expire after 24 hours.

Private account: if you turn it on in Settings, only people you accept can add you and see your posts, Stories and friends. Anyone can still find your name, username, profile photo and bio, and the people who already follow you stay. Your posts don't join Scenes and can't be reshared. When you turn it off, people who asked to add you become followers.

Scenes group public posts of the same moment, added by their authors. Only public posts can join, and anyone who can see your post can see it in the Scene, with your name, profile photo, the place name on your post and when it was filmed. The Scene shows who started it. You can remove your angle at any time; a post that is deleted or no longer public leaves every Scene.

If you're under 18: your posts and Stories reach only your followers in recommendations. They don't appear in Watch's For You, Trending, Happening now, topic pages, search, the map or Scenes for anyone who doesn't follow you, even if your account is public, and you aren't suggested to adults; people find your account by its exact username. What ULOOM recommends to you never includes sensitive posts, or accounts that people under 18 have blocked or reported. Until you give your birthday, ULOOM treats your account this way.

Limited posts and accounts: when our team limits a post, a Story or an account, it stays on the profile and in followers' Following, but isn't recommended, searched or shown on the map.

Hashtags and trends

  • Hashtags: a hashtag in any language links your post to that topic (up to five per post); different spellings of the same word, for example with or without Arabic vowel marks, are one topic.
  • Trending tags: we count how many different people use a tag in a day, in each country (from the country your phone was set to when you gave your birthday) and everywhere, and compare it with that tag's usual. Only public posts by adults whose accounts are at least a week old count, and a tag trends in a country only after someone on our team has checked it there. These are totals; they never show who posted.
  • Trending posts: counted by the different people who liked, commented on, reposted or saved a post, never by its author.
  • Trending in Discover: each approved trend shows its most-watched public posts from accounts at least a week old, and only posts you're allowed to see.
  • Our team can keep a tag out of Trending, hide it or block it, for example to stop spam or harmful content. Every decision is recorded with its reason.
  • Tags about suicide and self-harm: some are blocked, so posts don't link to them. Their page, and a search for them, show where to find support instead, including a link to Find A Helpline (findahelpline.com, by ThroughLine), an outside site with its own privacy policy. We don't keep a record that you opened that page, and such a search isn't added to your recent searches.

Reports and our team's decisions

ULOOM's team reviews reports. When someone reports a message, our team can see that message, including its photo, video or voice note, to review the report. They can't see the rest of the conversation. Automatic checks for things like spam look at what you post, comment and put in your profile, never at private messages.

When we act on something you posted or on your account, we tell you why in the app (Settings → Account status), and you can ask us to look again.

For security, we keep a record of sign-ins (time, network address and device) for six months.

Service providers

  • Supabase hosts our database, file storage and sign-in, in the EU (Frankfurt, Germany).
  • Expo and Apple deliver push notifications. Apple also distributes the beta through TestFlight.
  • Apple and Google handle sign-in if you choose to sign in with them.
  • Apple Maps turns your position into place names on your device when you tag a location.
  • OpenRouter passes requests from the Director in Formats to an AI model made by OpenAI, which writes the script. OpenRouter sends each request to a provider that runs that model, and we ask it to use only providers that don't keep or train on requests. If you use the Director, the topic you type, the template you picked and the language you chose go from our servers through OpenRouter to that provider; your name, account and location don't.

They process data only to provide these services to us.

Keeping and deleting your data

You can delete your own posts, stories, comments, archive items and messages at any time, and delete a chat or a message for yourself only. Deleting a post or story also deletes its photo and video files, unless the same file is still used somewhere else, such as your archive. Deleting your account in Settings removes your account, profile, content, archive, usage data, Watch records, what For You learned about you, Impact totals, your birthday and the days you used ULOOM from our database, and the photo and video files you uploaded. If you signed in with Apple, we also ask Apple to remove ULOOM from your Apple ID. Files uploaded with earlier beta versions and left after a deletion can be removed on request: email us. A record of reports and of our team's actions, including the username involved, may be kept after an account is deleted, for safety and legal reasons. Beta data may be wiped when the beta ends.

Your choices and rights

You can turn off location and notifications in your device settings, and "Count my visits", "Share usage data" and "Personalised For You" in ULOOM's Settings, at any time, and reset For You there. To get a copy of your data, use Download your data in Settings → Account: it saves one file with your account, profile, posts, Stories, archive, the messages you sent and your activity, with links to your photos, videos and voice notes. It doesn't include other people's messages to you. For a correction or deletion, or anything the download doesn't cover, email boonamazon@gmail.com.

Children

ULOOM is for people 13 and older, and older where the law says so (16 in Australia). We ask for your birthday to check this and don't knowingly keep data of anyone younger. If you're 13 to 17, a new account starts private, your posts reach only your followers in recommendations (see Who can see your content), your views are never part of anyone's age, country or time breakdowns, and For You never learns from what you watch.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and tell beta testers in the app or by email for any significant change.
