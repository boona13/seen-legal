How ULOOM recommends

Updated 8 October 2026

ULOOM suggests posts, people and tags so you find things worth your time. This page explains, in plain words, what decides what you see, how anything our team picks is labelled, and what you can change. The Privacy Policy covers what data this uses and how long it's kept.

For You

For You is Watch's main feed. For each post it could show you, it weighs these, most important first:

  1. Whether you may see it. Your blocks and mutes, posts you marked "Not interested", our team's limits and the rules for under-18s always come first. A post that fails any of them isn't shown.
  2. How people who saw it reacted. Watching to the end, sharing, saving, following the author, commenting, reposting and liking count for a post; "Not interested" and reports count strongly against it. These are totals, never who. A post with few views is judged close to the average until there's enough to go on, so a lucky or unlucky start doesn't decide it.
  3. What you're interested in. Who and which topics you follow, creators and topics you've liked, saved or commented on, and, if you're an adult with Personalised For You on, what you watch to the end or skip.
  4. How new it is. Newer posts come first; a post's weight halves about every day.
  5. Your country. Posts from the country your phone is set to rank a little higher.

Then it mixes the list so it stays varied: never two posts in a row from the same person, at most three in ten on one topic, and at most two in ten older posts. One place in ten goes to a new post being shown to a few people first, which reaches more people if they like it. A new person's first posts get a first audience of real people.

We never sell a better position, never use sensitive things about you, and never use where you are more precisely than your phone's country. The For You described above never uses follower counts or verified badges to push a post.

While we compare: some people still get the older For You, so we can check the new one does better before everyone gets it. The older one ranks the posts you may see by likes, comments, reposts and saves, who and which topics you follow, and how new a post is, and gives verified accounts a small lift (10%). It will be retired; this page will change when it is.

Following and Discover

  • Following shows the people you follow, newest first. Until you follow 10 people it adds posts you might like after your friends' ones, marked "Suggested for you".
  • Trending tags are counted from how many different people use them, in your country and everywhere, against their usual. A tag trends only after someone on our team has checked it; our team can't make a tag trend.
  • Rising creators are adults growing faster than their own usual, from real new followers. Our team checks each one before they're shown.
  • Suggested people each come with a reason, such as "Follows you" or "Followed by 3 you follow".

Featured by ULOOM and campaigns

Sometimes our team picks a post, a creator or a campaign to share with more people. They're always labelled:

  • Featured by ULOOM a post or creator our team picked;
  • ULOOM team accounts of people on our team, which are never featured with any other label;
  • ULOOM · Campaign name a campaign: a page gathering public posts with a few tags.

Limits we keep for every one of them:

  • In For You, one place in 20 at most, never two in a row. There, the same featured item reaches you twice at most, and a campaign twice a day at most.
  • Each runs for 30 days at most, is approved by someone on our team, and is recorded with its reason. Anything shown to under-18s needs a second person to approve it.
  • They're chosen by country, never from what you do. With Personalised For You off you get only those for everywhere.
  • They follow the same rules as everything else: your blocks, mutes and "Not interested", our team's limits, and the rules for under-18s.
  • Never political or election content, never a sensitive post, never anyone under 18, never a private account, and never anyone who turned off "Suggest my account to others". A campaign only shows posts that meet these rules.
  • Featured posts don't count toward Trending, featured creators aren't shown as rising, and a campaign's tags don't trend while it runs.

There are no ads or paid placements in ULOOM. If that ever changes, they'll be labelled "Sponsored" and this page will say so first.

Your choices

  • Personalised For You (Settings → Recommendations): turn it off and For You uses only who and which topics you follow, not what it learned about you or your country. What it learned is cleared.
  • Reset For You, in the same place, starts again from what you follow.
  • Not interested and Show less from @name are in each post's menu.
  • Why am I seeing this? In a post's menu in Watch, it shows the reasons that post was chosen for you.
  • Suggest my account to others (Settings → Recommendations): off, you aren't suggested to anyone, shown as rising or featured.
  • Mute or block anyone, from their profile or a post's menu.

If you're under 18

Your posts reach only your followers in recommendations, and you aren't suggested to adults. Your For You uses only what you choose (who and which topics you follow, what you like, save and comment on), never what you watch, and never shows sensitive posts or accounts that people under 18 have blocked or reported. Until you give your birthday, ULOOM treats your account this way.

What our team can do

  • Limit a post or an account under our Community Guidelines: it stays on the profile and for followers, but isn't recommended or searched. We tell you why in Settings → Account status, and you can ask us to look again. We can also hide or remove content that breaks the Guidelines.
  • Check trends and rising creators before they're shown, keep a tag out of Trending, or hide or block it.
  • Feature posts, creators and campaigns, labelled and limited as above.
  • Compare versions of For You using totals (what people did with what they were shown), and change its weights only by making a new version, never by editing one in place.
  • To help you or to check a report, see why a post was or wasn't shown to you. Every action and every look is recorded with who did it and why.
